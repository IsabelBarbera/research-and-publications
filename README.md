# Research & Publications
### AI Safety | Privacy | Security | AI Governance

Welcome! This repository brings together my research, publications, and contributions on AI safety, privacy, security, and responsible AI.

My work focuses on understanding and addressing risks in AI systems, from privacy and security vulnerabilities to autonomy, manipulation, fairness, and accountability. I'm particularly interested in AI safety engineering, developing methods and benchmarks to evaluate AI systems, and understanding how safety, privacy, and security can be built into AI systems throughout their lifecycle.
